YGPA Calculator

Enter the SGPA and total credits for each semester in the academic year (odd + even). YGPA is calculated using the credit-index formula.

MAKAUT formula: YGPA = (Credit Index of Odd Semester + Credit Index of Even Semester) ÷ (Total Odd Semester Credits + Total Even Semester Credits). Credit Index = SGPA × semester credits.

Formula: YGPA = [Σ(SGPA × semester credits) for odd + even semesters] ÷ [Σ odd-semester credits + Σ even-semester credits]. Use official semester credits.
